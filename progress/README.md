## 2D vision style guide — test frames

**Made by Leta**, August 2026. The visions are hand-drawn 2D sequences in a
strict three-colour palette — black `#120417`, white `#ffffff`, blood-red
`#b60c28`. These frames were drawn to settle how the red is allowed to behave
before anyone draws a finished vision. They are the rule the rest of the 2D art
has to follow.

| | |
|---|---|
| ![Hard-edged black, white and red split; red fills the highlighted object](style-frames/style-frame-01-object-hint.png) | **Object hint.** Hard-edge split between the three colours, with red filling the highlighted object itself. |
| ![A flat black silhouette of a figure standing in a red archway](style-frames/style-frame-02-person-hint.png) | **Person hint.** The figure stays a flat black silhouette and the red marks the space around them instead — one variation of the rule, not the only one. |
| ![A red form with gradient shading and a white highlight line](style-frames/style-frame-03-gradient-test.png) | **Gradient test.** Unlike the flat fills in the first two, this one tests whether gradient shading can add depth without breaking the flat noir look. |

## Still to add

the same two things: **who made it**, and **what in it is a placeholder**.

  of ours, and only pages that name no places or characters.

## Rules for anything added here

- Label every item with **who made it**. If it was a joint piece of work, say so.
- Say what is a **placeholder**, every time. A prototype still is not finished
  art, and should never be allowed to look like one.
- **Nothing third-party**: not the stock character or its animations, not the
  stock footage, not the mood references gathered off the internet. Not as files,
  and not as the subject of a screenshot.
- **Nothing that names the novel**, its author, its characters or its places.
- Keep files small — a short GIF or a compressed MP4, not a raw capture.
